Our Mission

FoodLoop is a frontend concept for a simple idea: good food should reach people, not landfills. We connect donors, farmers, markets and community organisations in one clear flow.

This is a static demo. No real donations, orders, payments or collections are processed.

What we do??

Feeding Lives

Every meal counts! Our platform connects surplus food from restaurants, grocery stores, and individuals with those in need. Join us in reducing food waste and feeding communities.

Protecting Earth

Food waste isn’t just a loss of meals—it’s a major contributor to climate change. By rescuing food, we reduce methane emissions, save water, and protect our planet.

Stronger Together

Stronger together! We unite donors, volunteers, and organizations to fight food waste. Be part of a movement that turns excess into impact!

How FoodLoop Works

Four simple steps from surplus to shared value.

  1. 1

    List surplus food

    A donor shares food type, quantity, location and pickup timing.

  2. 2

    We show nearby matches

    Community partners appear on a simple nearby map and list.

  3. 3

    Value is recovered

    Food reaches people or becomes an upcycled marketplace product.

  4. 4

    Impact is visible

    Demo totals show how much waste and loss could be avoided.
